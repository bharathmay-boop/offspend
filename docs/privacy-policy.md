# Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Offspend helps you track what you spend by reading the transaction messages your bank already sends you. It is built so that your financial information stays on your phone.

## What the app reads

- **Text messages (SMS)**, with your permission. When a message arrives, the app checks whether it is a bank, card, UPI or mobile money transaction. On first setup it also looks at the last 90 days of messages, and later it re-checks messages it may have missed while your phone had stopped it.
- **Notifications from payment and bank apps**, only if you turn this on. The app looks only at notifications from a fixed list of payment and banking apps and ignores notifications from every other app.

## What the app keeps

For each transaction it finds, the app stores on your phone: the amount, the currency, whether money went out or came in, the payee or payer as written in the message, the last digits of the account or card, the time, and the category and note you choose.

It does not keep the text of your messages. Messages that are not transactions, such as one-time passwords, personal messages and offers, are skipped and not stored.

## What leaves your phone

Nothing. The app does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your messages, your transactions or any statistics anywhere. There is no account and no server.

On phones that support it, the app may ask Google's on-device model (Gemini Nano, through Android's AICore service) to read a message its own rules could not. This runs inside your phone's operating system, and the message is not sent off the device by this app.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored.

## Children

The app is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children.
